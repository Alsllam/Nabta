## The mental shift

For a product whose user cannot read, cannot consent and cannot complain, **the non-goals are
the product**. "No open-ended chat", "no child accounts", "no retained recordings", "no streak
that can be lost" are not scope cuts to revisit when there is time; they are the safety
architecture, and each one traces to a principle. The second shift is smaller but changes the
plan: the first users are my own children. That makes the pilot start earlier (Stage 0, from
Phase 7) and makes the data rules stricter, not looser — their recordings are purged and their
profiles hold no surname exactly like anyone else's, otherwise the rules were never real.

## Gotchas hit

- A fenced div cannot change style mid-block, so the story is three divs — `RTL`, then
  `ChildText` for the three story lines, then `RTL` again. The Markdown reads a little broken;
  the Word output does not.
- Wide tables wrap badly in Word, so the goals table keeps its last column ("From") to two or
  three words and puts the measurement, the long column, third. Column order is a layout tool.
- The charter came out at ~3,400 words, above the 3–6 page habit. The goals and risks tables are
  what grew; both are reference material for the SRS and the pilot design, so they stay.
- No build errors: `{.unnumbered}` on the revision history and the appendix rendered as intended
  on the first pass, and harakat survived in `ChildText` (verified by reading the `.docx` back).
